Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an appointment, or do you take walk-ins? ↓

To ensure you receive dedicated time with our senior artists, we highly recommend booking an appointment in advance. Walk-ins are accommodated only if a slot is available.

Do you offer bridal makeup trials? ↓

Yes, we offer paid bridal trials in our studio. The cost of the trial is fully adjusted against your final bridal package if you choose to book with us.

Is there parking available near the salon? ↓

Valet parking is available at our Waterfield Road flagship location. Simply pull up to the entrance and our team will assist you.

What is your cancellation policy? ↓

We request at least 24 hours notice for any cancellations or rescheduling to avoid a cancellation fee, as we block significant time for major chemical treatments.
