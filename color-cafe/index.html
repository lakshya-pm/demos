Questions & Booking Policies

Do I need an appointment, or do you take walk-ins? ↓

To ensure you receive dedicated time with our senior artists without rushing, we highly recommend booking an appointment in advance. Walk-ins are accommodated only if a slot is available.

How much does bridal makeup cost? ↓

Bridal makeup packages start from ₹15,000 depending on the events, location, and the seniority of the artist. We provide custom quotes during your bridal consultation and trial.

Is there parking available near the salon? ↓

Yes, valet parking is available at our Waterfield Road flagship location. Simply pull up to the entrance and our team will securely park your vehicle.

What is your cancellation policy? ↓

We request at least 24 hours notice for any cancellations or rescheduling to avoid a fee, as we block significant dedicated time for major chemical treatments and bridal trials.
